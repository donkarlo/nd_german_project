Privacy Policy — NoonDreams German Learning

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Developer: NoonDreams

Contact: contact@noondreams.com

Website: https://noondreams.com

This policy describes how the public Android application German Learning (package com.ndgerman.android.play) handles information. It does not describe the developer's separate private desktop or Android application.

1. Dictionary and locally stored information

The public app provides an offline German dictionary and grammar reference. Dictionary queries, search history, preferences, and any local application state are stored on the device. The public app does not require a user account, does not synchronize dictionary data to Dropbox, and does not upload dictionary searches to a NoonDreams server.

The Android operating system may back up app data depending on the device owner's system backup settings. You may remove local application information using Android's app storage controls or by uninstalling the app, subject to device backups.

2. Advertising and consent

When the production app is configured to show advertising, the app integrates Google AdMob. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising identifiers, IP address, device information, diagnostics, and advertising interactions to serve, limit, prevent abuse of, and measure ads. The collection, sharing, retention and use of this information depend on Google's services and the user's privacy choices.

The app requests and uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent information where applicable. Where available, the user can review privacy options from the app menu. If advertisements have not been configured, the app does not request ads.

For more information about Google's data practices and privacy controls, visit Google Privacy Policy and Google AdMob Help.

3. In-app purchases

If you purchase the optional remove-ads feature when offered, billing and payment processing occur through Google Play. NoonDreams does not receive or store your complete payment card details through the app. The app checks Google Play purchase status to determine entitlement to the feature; local entitlement state may be stored on the device.

4. Text-to-speech

The app offers pronunciation using the Android text-to-speech service installed or selected on the device. The text-to-speech provider may process text or audio requests under its own policies and device settings.

5. Data security and retention

The public dictionary data is bundled into the app. App preferences and history are stored using Android local storage. The developer does not operate a server that stores public-app dictionary search history. Information handled by Google's SDKs is subject to Google's technical and retention practices.

6. Children

This app is a general language-learning reference and is not specifically designed for children. Advertising configuration and Google Play audience declarations must reflect the intended age group before launch.

7. Your choices and contact

You can manage locally stored information through Android app storage controls. When advertising privacy options are offered in the app, use the menu to review consent choices. For privacy questions, contact contact@noondreams.com.

We may revise this policy if features, advertising, or data practices change. The current public version will be available from the app's Google Play listing and our website.
