## Full description (English)
Explore German vocabulary with a searchable offline dictionary and practical grammar reference.

• Look up German vocabulary with English and Persian meanings and Penglish transliterations.
• Study pronunciation, examples and grammatical information included with entries.
• Practice common verb forms, conjugations and German grammatical structures.
• Consult articles, pronouns, adjective endings and noun forms.
• Use the dictionary and grammar reference without an internet connection.

German Learning is a reference app by NoonDreams. The public version is read-only, does not require an account, and does not access Dropbox.
